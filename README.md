## 🌊 What I'm building right now
 
### [Forecasting Harmful Algal Bloom Risk](https://github.com/Break-Through-Tech/Biointerphase-1B-forecasting-harmful-algal-bloom-risk)
**Break Through Tech AI Studio × Biointerphase**
 
Harmful algal blooms poison marine life, close beaches, and make people sick — and right now they're mostly caught *after* they've already formed. My team is training a model to flag bloom risk before that happens, using water quality, weather, and satellite data from Florida's coast.
 
I own the **random forest model and feature engineering** across 7 NOAA and USF datasets — about 750,000 rows of genuinely messy real-world data, which has taught me more than any clean tutorial dataset ever did.
